# Privacy Policy for Auduet

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

Auduet is an audio playback application designed to let users listen to audio
and podcasts alongside their music.

## Information We Collect

Auduet does not require users to create an account.

Auduet does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Playback preferences, imported audio files, and library information may be
stored locally on your device to provide the application's functionality.

## Podcasts and External Services

When you access podcasts or other online audio content, Auduet may connect to
external servers to retrieve audio files, podcast feeds, and artwork.

Those servers may receive technical information associated with network
requests, such as your IP address. Their data handling practices are governed by
their respective privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

Auduet does not sell personal information to third parties.

## Data Retention

Information stored locally by Auduet can generally be removed by deleting the
application and its associated data from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Auduet is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Auduet's features or data practices
change.
